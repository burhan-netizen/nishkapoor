Meet Nish

More than selling homes. It's about relationships.

I'm a long-time Ottawa resident and a full-time Realtor with Royal LePage. I studied business with a finance major at the University of Ottawa's Telfer School of Management, and spent years in marketing and sales before real estate. I bring all of it to every deal: the numbers, the strategy and the hustle.

But what keeps me going is the people. A newcomer who almost gave up on owning in Canada and now has keys in hand. A young family who got five bedrooms after a tough renegotiation. Those are my best days.

How I work

Buy smart. Sell strong. Invest wisely.

Your real estate journey deserves the right strategy. Here's how we get you there, with no pressure and no overselling, just honest advice.

01

Buy Smart

I learn what you're really looking for first, then we hunt. I book showings fast (sometimes within hours of a listing going live), research every neighbourhood and negotiate hard to keep you within budget.

  • First-time buyers
  • Neighbourhood research
  • Offer strategy
  • Budget-first negotiation
02

Sell Strong

Fact-based pricing, staging advice that makes buyers fall in love, and marketing that fills your showings every day until offer night. The goal is top dollar with a smooth close.

  • Pricing strategy
  • Staging & home prep
  • Premium marketing
  • Offer-night negotiation
03

Invest Wisely

Pre-construction vs. resale, rental potential, ROI from day one. I'll show you how Ottawa's market is shifting and where the smart money is moving, so you're building long-term wealth.

  • Investment property
  • Pre-construction vs. resale
  • Upsizing & downsizing
  • Rentals
